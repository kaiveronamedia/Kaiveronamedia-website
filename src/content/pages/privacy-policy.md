## Who processes your data

- **Web3Forms** delivers enquiries sent through the contact form to our email inbox.
- **Cal.com** handles bookings made on the booking page.
- **Cloudflare** hosts this website and provides cookieless Web Analytics.
- **YouTube**, via youtube-nocookie.com, plays videos on this site. Nothing loads from YouTube until you click play.
- **The ROI calculator** at roi.kaiverona.co.uk collects the details you enter there and passes them to **Brevo**.

## Contact
